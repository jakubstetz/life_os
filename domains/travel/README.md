# Domain: travel

**Purpose.** Track upcoming (and past) **flights**.

## How I work with it

I track travel in a consolidated space so that I have a single efficient view for seeing core travel dates and details across various providers.

When I know I will be traveling on certain dates, I create a data row for that with just the dates and locations filled in, so that I know that the reservation still needs to be booked. Once I've booked the reservation, I fill in the rest of the fields.

A **trip** is an organizing container for multiple related flights.

### Purchasing with dollars vs points

When booking new travel, I look at the reservation's dollar cost and points cost and fill both out in my data, so that I can calculate the dollar value per point for that reservation. I compare that against the average point value reported by rewards blogs (e.g. NerdWallet, The Points Guy) to determine whether I should purchase the reservation with dollars or points.

- If the calculated dollar-value-per-point is more than the average reported value, I'll tend to purchase the reservation with points because I'm getting a good deal on the points.
- If the calculated dollar-value-per-point is less than the average reported value, I'll tend to purchase with dollars to save points for a better deal later.

### Company-reimbursable travel

I track what travel is connected with my full-time work so that I don't miss submitting reimbursements for such travel.

### Personal LLC travel

I track what travel is tied to my personal LLC so that I don't miss filing these travels as business expenses. Also, even just the existence of this field reminds me to use my business card for purchasing these tickets rather than forgetting and using my personal card. This generally includes any travel done to attend, staff, or facilitate Satvatove seminars.

### Family business  travel

I track what travel is tied to my family's business so that I don't miss filing these travels as business expenses. Also, even just the existence of this field reminds me to use a business card for purchasing these tickets rather than forgetting and using my personal card. This generally includes any travel I do to the family property to work there, chat with mom and dad about the work that's happening and finances/admin/legal, etcetera.
