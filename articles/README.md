# Articles

Essays capturing the core philosophical and technical threads behind Life OS.

These are not architecture documents (those describe *how the system is built*) and not daily notes. They are **first-principles writing** — the foundational ideas, their nuances, and their implications — meant for two audiences:

- **Me**, as a place to codify and return to my own thinking.
- **Future agents**, as authoritative context for *why* the system is shaped the way it is. When an agent must make a judgment call that the architecture docs don't directly answer, these articles are the priors.

Each article states a thesis, develops it, names its tensions honestly, and ends with a short distilled set of principles for quick reference and extraction.

## Index

- [Fluency in the Digital Substrate](fluency-in-the-digital-substrate.md) — Why understanding how the digital world works is itself a form of agency — the pre-AI root that AI multiplies, and the soil the other essays grow from.
- [The AI-Pilled Engineer](the-ai-pilled-engineer.md) — Why a capable engineer can now own, rather than rent, the software that runs their life — and where that stops being true.
- [Proportionate Privacy](proportionate-privacy.md) — A privacy stance for the AI era: act while the window is open, share shape not substance, route by sensitivity — proportionate, not paranoid.
- [Why a Life OS](why-a-life-os.md) — What a personal operating system is for, and why agents are what finally make it viable.
- [Open-Format Sovereignty](open-format-sovereignty.md) — Why the source of truth must live in open, durable formats you own (Markdown for prose, JSON/CSV/SQLite for data), and every intelligence layer above it is merely a derived, disposable view.
